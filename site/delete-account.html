Delete account

刪除微縮台灣的帳號

App 名稱：微縮台灣（英文名 Islandia）· 開發者：AirNoir · Android 套件 com.airnoir.islandia · 最後更新：2026-11-09

這是「微縮台灣」的帳號與資料刪除說明，適用 iOS 與 Android。在 App 裡刪除會立刻清掉雲端帳號與收藏備份。沒有帶著手機時，可以寄信要求刪除。

在 App 裡刪除

「刪除帳號」在你用 Apple 或 Google 登入之後才會出現。步驟如下：

  1. 打開微縮台灣。
  2. 點下方的「我的」。
  3. 若還沒登入，先在「備份」用當初的帳號登入。iOS 可以用 Apple，或按「使用 Google 繼續」；Android 按「使用 Google 繼續」。
  4. 在同一個「備份」區塊按「刪除帳號」，再按「刪除」確認。

確認後，App 會立刻呼叫雲端的 delete-account，刪除這個帳號和它的雲端收藏備份，無法復原。登出不會刪除帳號，雲端備份還在。

只用匿名帳號、從未用 Apple 或 Google 登入的話，畫面上不會有這個按鈕。那種帳號在 30 天沒有使用後會自動刪除；刪掉 App 會清掉手機上的資料。

沒有帶著 App 時

寄信到 support@littlestep.me，主旨寫「請刪除微縮台灣帳號」。請用你登入用的電子郵件寄出（或在信裡寫上那個電子郵件），並註明是 Apple 還是 Google。我們確認是你本人之後，會刪除與 App 內相同的那一批雲端資料。

這是支援頁已經在用的同一個信箱，沒有另外的申請表單。

會立刻刪除的資料

  • Supabase 上的帳號本身（匿名、Apple 或 Google）。
  • 雲端收藏備份：解鎖過哪些景點、時間、順序、當時的季節與天氣。
  • 為了營運後台而記的裝置與活躍日、以及產品使用事件（例如打卡、打開付費牆）。這些列跟著帳號一起刪除。

以上在你於 App 內確認的當下就刪除，沒有額外的保留期。以電子郵件要求時，在我們確認身份後做同樣的刪除。

會保留的資料，以及保留多久

  • 這支手機上的收藏和照片。刪除帳號不會清掉它們。照片從未上傳。要連手機上的資料一起移除，請刪除 App。
  • 你已經送出的問題回報。回報會留下，以便我們修正問題，但不再連結到這個帳號。若回報裡留了回覆用電子郵件，那封信箱地址會留在該則回報中。回報沒有自動到期日；若要連某一則回報一起刪掉，請在同一封信裡說明。
  • 購買紀錄。一次買斷的完整版（商品 com.airnoir.islandia.full）由 Apple App Store 或 Google Play 處理，並由 RevenueCat 確認購買狀態。我們拿不到你的付款卡號。刪除帳號不會退款，也不會抹掉商店或 RevenueCat 依其政策保存的交易紀錄。之後用同一個商店帳號仍可在 App 內「恢復購買」。
  • 從未登入的匿名帳號。最後一次使用後滿 30 天會自動刪除，連同它的雲端收藏備份。

English

Delete your Islandia account

App: 微縮台灣 (Islandia) · Developer: AirNoir · Android package com.airnoir.islandia · Last updated: 2026-11-09

This page explains how to delete an Islandia account on iOS or Android. Deleting in the app removes the cloud account and its backup immediately. You can also ask by email if you do not have the app.

Delete in the app

“Delete account” appears after you sign in with Apple or Google:

  1. Open 微縮台灣.
  2. Open the Me tab.
  3. If you are not signed in, sign in on the Backup card with the same account. On iOS that is Apple, or “Continue with Google”; on Android it is “Continue with Google”.
  4. On that card, tap Delete account, then confirm.

The app then calls the delete-account service and deletes the account and its cloud backup at once. This cannot be undone. Signing out does not delete the account; the cloud backup stays.

If you never signed in with Apple or Google, there is no button. That anonymous account is deleted automatically after 30 days without use, and deleting the app clears what is on the phone.

Without the app

Email support@littlestep.me with the subject “Delete my Islandia account”. Write from the email you signed in with, or include it, and say whether it was Apple or Google. After we confirm it is you, we delete the same cloud data as the in-app action.

This is the support address already published on the support page. There is no separate form.

Deleted immediately

  • The Supabase account (anonymous, Apple or Google).
  • The cloud backup of your collection: which spots, when, in what order, and the season and weather at the time.
  • Device and active-day rows, and product events (such as a check-in or opening the paywall), which are removed with the account.

In the app this happens as soon as you confirm, with no extra retention period. An email request does the same deletion once we have confirmed it is you.

What is kept, and for how long

  • The collection and photos on this phone. Deleting the account leaves them in place. Photos were never uploaded. Delete the app to remove what is on the device.
  • Problem reports you already sent. We keep the report so we can fix the issue, but it is no longer linked to the account. A reply email left on the report stays with that report. Reports have no automatic expiry; ask in the same email if you want a specific report deleted too.
  • Purchase records. The full version (com.airnoir.islandia.full) is a one-time purchase processed by the Apple App Store or Google Play, and confirmed by RevenueCat. We never see your card number. Deleting the account does not refund you or erase the transaction records those companies keep under their own policies. You can still restore the purchase in the app with the same store account.
  • Anonymous accounts that never signed in. Removed automatically 30 days after the last use, including their cloud backup.

See the Privacy Policy for everything else we collect.
