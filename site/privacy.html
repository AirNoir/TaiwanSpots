微縮台灣 Islandia 隱私權政策

最後更新：2026-11-09 · 適用於 iOS 與 Android App「微縮台灣」（英文名 Islandia，開發者 AirNoir，Android 套件 com.airnoir.islandia）

微縮台灣是一個用定位打卡、收藏台灣景點微縮模型的 App。這份政策說明我們收集什麼、為什麼、放在哪裡，以及你怎麼刪掉它。iOS 與 Android 適用同一份政策；兩邊做法不同的地方會標出來。

我們收集什麼

位置資訊

只在你使用 App 的時候讀取（iOS 為「使用期間」；Android 為大致與精確定位，沒有背景定位），用來判斷你是否抵達景點。iOS 另用於主畫面小工具和鎖定畫面顯示最近的景點距離；Android 這次沒有小工具。打卡判斷在你的裝置上完成。我們的伺服器不接收你的 GPS 座標，也不保存位置軌跡。

地圖

App 內的地圖只用來標出景點，不在 App 裡算路線。iOS 的底圖是 Apple 地圖。Android 的底圖使用 Google Maps SDK for Android：裝置會向 Google 索取你正在看的地圖圖磚。你允許定位時，地圖上的藍點由該地圖 SDK 繪製，適用 Google 或 Apple 各自的政策。點「導航」只是把景點的公開座標交給你手機上的地圖 App。

收藏紀錄

你解鎖過哪些景點、解鎖時間與順序、當時的季節與天氣。儲存在你的裝置上（iOS 另存在 App 與小工具共用的 App Group）。

帳號與備份

App 第一次開啟時會在背景建立一個匿名 Supabase 帳號，把收藏紀錄備份到我們的雲端（Supabase，資料存放於東京）。備份不含位置，也不含照片。

你可以在「我的」頁把備份綁到自己的帳號：iOS 可用「透過 Apple 登入」或 Google 登入；Android 用 Google 登入（Android 不提供 Apple 登入）。此時我們只會收到該帳號提供的電子郵件（Apple 可選擇隱藏）與一組識別碼，用來讓你換手機時找回收藏。

用 Google 帳號登入時

Google 登入只要求基本的個人資料範圍（電子郵件、識別碼）。我們不會存取你的 Google 聯絡人、雲端硬碟、日曆或其他 Google 資料，也不會把 Google 帳號資料分享給廣告商。電子郵件只用來識別帳號，不寄行銷信。

照片與相機

你在景點頁加入的照片（每座最多 3 張）只儲存在你的手機裡，不會上傳，也不在雲端備份裡。App 存檔時會重新壓縮，不保留原始照片的拍攝位置等中繼資料。相機是選擇性的，只有在你要拍照時才詢問。

  • iOS：從相簿選照片，或把分享卡存進相簿時，才會詢問相簿權限。
  • Android：從相簿挑選使用系統的照片選擇器，不申請讀取整個相簿的權限。「當天拍的照片」和「在這附近拍的照片」只在 iOS 提供。

產品使用與裝置資訊

為了知道 App 是否正常、哪些功能有人在用，App 會把少量紀錄寫進我們自己的 Supabase（東京），不是第三方分析服務：

  • 每天第一次打開或回到 App 時：平台（iOS 或 Android）、裝置型號、系統版本、App 版本、語言、是否為開發版、是否已購買完整版、手機上的收藏數量。
  • 你使用某些功能時：完成導覽、打卡（景點編號，不含座標）、看付費牆、購買或恢復購買的結果、分享、加入照片、打開導航。

這些紀錄不含位置、照片或廣告識別碼，也不用來投放廣告或賣給他人。帳號刪除時會跟著刪除。

回報問題與建議

只有在你主動按「回報問題・建議」送出時，我們才會收到：你寫的內容、選填的回覆用電子郵件、相關景點（如有），以及 App 版本、系統版本、裝置類型、語言、是否為完整版、已收藏數量。不含位置、照片或裝置識別碼。回報存在 Supabase（東京），只有我們看得到；電子郵件只用來回覆該則回報。

天氣查詢

為了顯示景點與各區域目前的天氣，App 會向 Open-Meteo（open-meteo.com）查詢「景點本身」和「北、中、南、東四個城市中心」的固定座標。這些是公開地標座標，不是你的位置；請求不含任何帳號或裝置識別資訊，結果會在裝置上快取 30 分鐘。

我們不做什麼

  • 不收集姓名。登入用的電子郵件只用來識別帳號，回報時留的電子郵件只用來回覆該則回報，都不用於行銷。
  • 不使用廣告 SDK，也不使用第三方分析 SDK（沒有把使用狀況送到 Firebase、Amplitude 這類服務）。上面的產品紀錄寫在我們自己的資料庫。
  • 不把你的 GPS 座標上傳到我們的伺服器。

App 內購買

完整版是一次買斷，商品編號 com.airnoir.islandia.full，不是訂閱。iOS 由 Apple 的 App Store 收款，Android 由 Google Play 帳務（Google Play Billing）收款，兩邊都透過 RevenueCat 確認你是否已購買。我們不會看到你的付款卡號。RevenueCat 只會收到商店提供的匿名交易資訊，以及一組由購買 SDK 產生的隨機使用者識別碼，不含電子郵件或位置。

資料刪除

用 Apple 或 Google 登入後，在「我的」→「備份」按「刪除帳號」，會立刻刪除雲端帳號、收藏備份，以及上面的裝置與產品使用紀錄。步驟、沒有帶 App 時如何寄信、以及哪些資料會留下，寫在刪除帳號。

刪除帳號不會清掉這支手機上的收藏和照片；刪除 App 才會。沒有登入過的匿名帳號，在 30 天沒有使用後會自動刪除。你已經送出的問題回報會留下，但不再連結到帳號。商店與 RevenueCat 上的購買紀錄依他們的政策保留。

政策更新

有變動時會更新本頁並修改上方日期。重大變更會在 App 內提示。

聯絡

有任何問題，請寄信到 support@littlestep.me，或在 App 的「我的」頁按「回報問題・建議」。

Islandia Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-11-09 · Applies to the iOS and Android app “微縮台灣 / Islandia” by AirNoir (Android package com.airnoir.islandia)

Islandia unlocks miniature dioramas of Taiwanese landmarks when you check in at them. This page explains what we collect, why, where it lives, and how to delete it. The same policy covers iOS and Android; where the two differ, it says so.

What we collect

Location

Read only while you are using the app (iOS: While Using; Android: approximate and precise location, never background), to decide whether you have reached a spot. On iOS it also shows the nearest spot on the home-screen widget and the lock screen; this Android release has no widget. The check-in decision is made on your device. Our servers never receive your GPS coordinates, and we do not keep a location trail.

Maps

The in-app map only marks spots. It does not calculate a route. The iOS base map is Apple Maps. The Android base map uses the Google Maps SDK for Android, so the device requests map tiles from Google for the area you are viewing. When you allow location, the blue dot is drawn by that map SDK under Google’s or Apple’s own policy. Tapping “Navigate” only hands the spot’s public coordinates to the maps app on your phone.

Collection progress

Which spots you have unlocked, when, in what order, and the season and weather at the time. Stored on your device (and, on iOS, in the App Group shared with the widget).

Account and backup

On first launch the app silently creates an anonymous Supabase account and backs up your collection to our cloud (Supabase, hosted in Tokyo). The backup does not include location or photos.

On the Me tab you can attach that backup to your own account: Sign in with Apple or Google on iOS, Google Sign-In on Android (Android has no Apple sign-in). We then receive only the email that account shares (Apple lets you hide it) and an identifier, so you can bring the passport back on a new phone.

When you sign in with Google

Google Sign-In requests only the basic profile scopes (email and identifier). We do not access your Google contacts, Drive, Calendar or any other Google data, and we do not share Google account data with advertisers. The email identifies the account; we send no marketing mail.

Photos and camera

Photos you add to a spot (up to 3 each) are stored only on your phone. They are never uploaded and are not part of the cloud backup. The app re-encodes them when saving, so the original’s metadata (such as where it was taken) is not kept. The camera is optional and is requested only when you choose to take a photo.

  • On iOS, photo-library access is requested only when you pick a photo or save a share card to Photos.
  • On Android, picking a photo uses the system photo picker. The app does not request permission to read your whole library. “Photos from that day” and “photos taken nearby” are iOS only.

Product use and device information

To see that the app is working and which features people use, the app writes a little information to our own Supabase project (Tokyo). This is not a third-party analytics service:

  • Once each day, when you open the app or return to it: platform (iOS or Android), device model, OS version, app version, language, whether it is a development build, whether you have the full version, and how many spots are collected on the phone.
  • When you use certain features: finishing onboarding, a check-in (the spot’s id, not coordinates), viewing the paywall, the result of a purchase or restore, sharing, adding a photo, or opening directions.

These records contain no location, photos or advertising id. They are not used for ads and are not sold. They are deleted with the account.

Problem reports and suggestions

Only when you choose to send one from “Report a problem or suggest” do we receive what you wrote, an optional reply email, the related spot (if any), and the app version, OS version, device type, language, whether you have the full version and how many spots you have collected. No location, photos or device identifiers. Reports are stored in Supabase (Tokyo) and seen only by us; the email is used only to reply to that report.

Weather lookups

To show current weather for spots and regions, the app queries Open-Meteo (open-meteo.com) for the fixed coordinates of the spot itself and of four city centres (north, central, south, east). These are public landmark coordinates, not your location; the request carries no account or device identifier, and results are cached on the device for 30 minutes.

What we do not do

  • No names. A sign-in email serves only to identify your account, and an email left on a report is used only to reply to it; neither is used for marketing.
  • No advertising SDKs, and no third-party analytics SDKs (nothing is sent to services such as Firebase Analytics or Amplitude). The product records above stay in our own database.
  • We do not upload your GPS coordinates to our servers.

In-app purchase

The full version is a one-time purchase, product id com.airnoir.islandia.full, not a subscription. On iOS, Apple’s App Store charges you. On Android, Google Play Billing charges you. Both are confirmed through RevenueCat. We never see your card number. RevenueCat receives only the store’s anonymised transaction data and a random user id created by the purchases SDK, never your email or location.

Deleting your data

After you sign in with Apple or Google, “Delete account” under Me → Backup removes the cloud account, the collection backup, and the device and product-use records at once. The steps, how to ask by email without the app, and what is kept are on Delete account.

Deleting the account does not clear the collection or photos on that phone; deleting the app does. Anonymous accounts that never signed in are removed automatically after 30 days without use. Problem reports you already sent are kept, but no longer linked to the account. Purchase records held by the stores and by RevenueCat follow their policies.

Changes

We update this page and the date above when anything changes. Material changes are announced in the app.

Contact

Email support@littlestep.me, or use “Report a problem or suggest” on the Me tab in the app.
